Project Overview

Key Business Insight: Symptom severity and comorbid mental health conditions show stronger associations with OCD outcomes than demographic factors alone.

This project explores a comprehensive clinical dataset of patients diagnosed with Obsessive-Compulsive Disorder (OCD), covering demographic attributes, symptom severity scores, and co-occurring mental health conditions.

The primary objective was to analyze relationships between demographic variables, clinical severity (Y-BOCS scores), and associated conditions such as anxiety and depression.

"The analysis reveals meaningful patterns between symptom duration, severity scores, and treatment pathways."

Through structured exploratory analysis and statistical visualization, the project provides insights that can support clinical research and data-driven mental health assessments.
